## Scope

This file lists the recurring filings, meetings and renewals a Saudi entity must complete each year, indexed first by entity type (LLC — شركة ذات مسؤولية محدودة; JSC — شركة مساهمة; simplified JSC — شركة مساهمة مبسطة; branch of a foreign company — فرع شركة أجنبية; professional company — شركة مهنية) and then by authority. It covers the Ministry of Commerce commercial-register cycle (annual confirmation replaces expiry under the 1446H Law), the Companies Law general-assembly, financial-statement and auditor rules for each form, and — as tagged model knowledge — ZATCA, GOSI, Qiwa/HRSD, chamber, municipality, MISA, UBO and Saudi Business Center events. It does not cover listed-company continuing obligations beyond one flag row, sector-regulator licences (SAMA, CMA, CST, SFDA), or one-off incorporation steps. Dates are Gregorian in this file; the authorities publish Hijri and Gregorian dates and ZATCA deadlines follow the entity's own fiscal year. **Do not apply a row to an entity type it does not name.**

## Open questions for a local practitioner

- **Annual-confirmation due date.** Commercial Register Law Art. 11 delegates the timing and form of the annual confirmation to the Regulations, which were not read. Confirm whether the due date is the registration anniversary, a fixed calendar date, or tied to the fiscal year, and whether the Ministry has issued any transitional rule for registrations that carried an old-law expiry date when the Law took effect on 2 April 2025.
- **Companies Law publication date.** The portal file's publication field was blank; confirm publication on 22/12/1443H and entry into force on 19 January 2023.
- **Deposit mechanism and micro/small thresholds.** Companies Law Arts. 17, 122, 147, 167 and 238 all say "deposit as the Regulations specify"; confirm that Qawaem is the deposit channel for LLCs, JSCs, simplified JSCs and branches alike, whether branches and micro/small companies without auditors deposit unaudited statements, and the current revenue/headcount bands for the audit exemption.
- **Simplified JSC annual meeting.** Art. 147 requires presentation of the statements to shareholders within six months but sets no assembly; confirm whether the Ministry treats a circulated written resolution as sufficient and what it expects deposited.
- **UBO Rules.** Confirm the Rules' issue instrument, effective date, the 25% threshold, the 15-day update period, whether the annual UBO confirmation is a separate filing or part of the CR annual confirmation, and the fine schedule.
- **ZATCA.** Confirm the 120-day return deadline for branches and for entities with a non-calendar fiscal year, the current VAT monthly/quarterly boundary (SAR 40 million), the WHT monthly deadline, the current e-invoicing wave schedule, and the zakat-payer transfer-pricing thresholds — all from memory.
- **GOSI.** Confirm the current contribution rates under the 2024 Social Insurance Law for employees hired after its effective date versus those under the previous law, the registration deadline for new hires, and the 15th-of-month payment date.
- **Qiwa / HRSD.** Confirm the expatriate levy amounts, whether work-permit renewal remains annual for all categories, the WPS upload deadline, and the current Nitaqat bands for the target's activity and size.
- **Chamber and municipality.** Confirm whether chamber subscription is still linked in any way to CR services after the recent decoupling, and whether the municipal licence term can exceed one year for the target's activity.
- **Foreign-ownership trigger for MISA.** Confirm at what level of non-GCC ownership a Saudi LLC or JSC is treated as a foreign investor required to hold a registration and file the annual update.

## How skills should use this file

- **`entity-compliance`** (corporate-legal): this is the primary consumer. At the tracker-build step, for each entity, first fix the entity type and fiscal year end (Art. 16 row), then pull every row whose Entity type column names that type or says "all".
