## Scope

The Law regulates the trade name (الاسم التجاري): the name a merchant uses in carrying on its commercial business to distinguish it from other merchants (Art. 1). It covers the duty to adopt and register a trade name, temporary reservation, permitted composition (including non-Arabic names), prohibited and confusingly similar names, exclusivity and compensation, display, amendment, disposal of the name separately from or together with the business, cancellation and deletion, and penalties. It applies to every merchant as defined by the Commercial Law. It does not govern trademarks (Trademarks Law and the Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property), company names as such under the Companies Law, or domain names; Art. 7(1)(b) is the only bridge to trademark law. The Regulation, not read, carries the similarity criteria, reservation period, and non-Arabic name rules.

## Open questions for a local practitioner

- The Implementing Regulation (Art. 22) was not read. It carries the reservation period (Art. 3(2)), the non-Arabic name rules and the restrictions on "Saudi", place names and family names (Art. 4(3)), the similarity and distinction criteria (Art. 7(1)(a)), the conditions for disposing of a name without the business (Art. 10(1)), and the offence table (Art. 14(3)).
- Royal Order 39929 of 28/6/1443H, which the Council of Ministers directed the Ministry to observe in drafting the Regulation (Resolution 237 item Sixth), was not read; its subject is unknown.
- Whether the Art. 6(2) damages claim is available to a merchant whose name is only reserved (the text says "registered") and whether the competent court is the commercial court.
- How Art. 11 (successor liability, five-year limitation) interacts with the Civil Transactions Law rules on assignment of debt and with a share deal (where the trade name does not "pass"); the text speaks only of transfer of the name "with the business".
- The Ministry's grandfathering terms for pre-2025 trade names (Royal Decree item Fourth).
- Effective date: 2 or 3 April 2025 (see Instrument table).
