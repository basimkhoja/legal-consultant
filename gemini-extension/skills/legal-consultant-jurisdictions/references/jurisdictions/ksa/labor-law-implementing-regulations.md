## Scope

The Regulations execute the Labor Law article by article (each regulation article opens with "في تنفيذ أحكام المادة ... من النظام") and attach five annexes that are themselves binding templates: the unified work regulations that every establishment must adopt or adapt (Annex 1), the accommodations schedule for workers with disabilities (Annex 2), the licensing rules for placement of Saudis (Annex 3) and for recruitment and labor-services companies (Annex 4), and the unified employment-contract forms that Qiwa generates (Annex 5). They cover casual/temporary contracts, work-regulation approval, records, Saudization and Nitaqat, restricted professions, recruitment and transfer of services, training, contract documentation, probation, relocation, overtime and compensatory leave, exempt categories, public holidays, unpaid and sick leave, part-time and flexible work, work-injury reporting, first-aid, remote sites, nursing breaks, minors, and the mechanics of imposing, contesting and settling HRSD fines.

They do not contain: the Wage Protection System rules (only referenced: Law Art. 90(2) as amended, Annex 1 Art. 15, Annex 5 cl. 9.1.2/11.1, Annex 4 Art. 63), the ministerial schedule of violations and penalties (referenced by Art. 38), the Nitaqat program rules (referenced throughout Art. 14), the friendly-settlement rules for labor disputes, the domestic-workers regulation, or the substantive end-of-service and termination rules, which remain in the Labor Law itself (Arts. 74–88). Where a row below states a Labor Law rule it is because the Regulations quote or depend on it.

## Open questions for a local practitioner

- Commencement: settled 2026-09-05. Item (fourth) of Ministerial Decision 115921 (read by OCR) puts the Regulations into effect from the date the Labor Law amendments took effect, 20 Sha'ban 1446H; the Official Gazette issue in which the Regulations were published was not read.
- Schedule of violations and penalties: the Regulations (Art. 38) operate on a separate ministerial decision. The HRSD page lists a revised schedule and the amendment-watch report claims a further revision effective 25 February 2026. Neither text was provided; every fine amount a skill needs is therefore `[model knowledge — verify]`.
- Wage Protection System: the Regulations only reference the program (Annex 1 Art. 15, Annex 5 cl. 9.1.2/11.1, Annex 4 Art. 63). The WPS decision(s) — coverage thresholds, monthly file deadline, compliance percentage, tiered service suspensions and fines, and the Mudad platform rules — were not provided.
- Resignation definition: Law Art. 2 (M/44) defines resignation by reference to a fixed-term contract, while Art. 74(3 bis) and Annex 1 Art. 57(4) list resignation as a general termination ground and Art. 75 still governs indefinite contracts. How labor courts treat a "resignation" from an indefinite contract that is submitted without 30 days' notice (Art. 79 bis clock versus Art. 75 notice and Art. 76 compensation) is unresolved on the text.
- Art. 14 Second (21) versus Law Art. 77: whether an expatriate who transfers after 12 months on 90 days' notice from a fixed-term contract owes Art. 77(2) remainder-of-term compensation, and whether contracts may waive it.
- Reg. Art. 4(1): whether an establishment under 50 workers that already adopted the model must switch to the lawyer route when it later adds any clause, and which law firms are "accredited on the Ministry's website".
- Flexible work: the penalty reference is to Ministerial Decision 178743 of 27/9/1440H "and its amendments" — not provided.
- Remote work: no article in the Regulations. The HRSD remote-work program rules, if any still apply, were not provided.
- Nitaqat: every Saudization threshold in Art. 14 depends on the Nitaqat program rules (a new phase is claimed from 26 April 2026 with the yellow band removed); the HRSD Nitaqat guide in the scratchpad was not read for this file.
- Annex 1 violations tables 2 and 3, Annex 2, Annex 3 and most of Annex 4 were skimmed, not read row by row.
- The 2023 English translation figure "27 hours per year" in Art. 22 versus the Arabic 720: confirm there is no intermediate decision that changed the annual overtime cap between 2023 and April 2025.
- Occupational fitness examinations regulation (Ministerial Decision 33232 of 11/3/1447H) and the project-management Saudization decision (70% from 14 February 2027) are listed on the HRSD page but were not read.
