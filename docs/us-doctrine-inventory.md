# US doctrine inventory — commercial-legal, corporate-legal, employment-legal

Phase 1 deliverable. Audit of upstream `anthropics/claude-for-legal` at commit `4a6c651` (fork branch `multi-jurisdiction`, audited 2026-09-04). Read-only: nothing in the plugins was changed in this phase. This file is the checklist Phase 3 works against; `docs/doctrine-coverage.md` (Phase 2) maps each row to the jurisdiction reference file that replaces it.

Three tables per plugin, then two cross-plugin tables:

1. **Doctrine** — every place a US-specific rule, statute, agency, state list, entity type, filing calendar, or research connector is assumed. Action is one of **replace** (operative US doctrine to swap for the applicable jurisdiction file), **branch** (neutral structure, US content; keep the US path and add a profile-driven branch), **leave** (illustrative text, placeholder hints, shared guardrails that must not be deleted, or the privilege-header discussion that already handles non-US).
2. **Cold-start interview questions** that assume a US practice, with the neutral or jurisdiction-driven replacement Phase 3 should write.
3. **Config paths** — every hard-coded `~/.claude/plugins/config/claude-for-legal/` reference Phase 4 must make runtime-neutral.

Row IDs: `C-` commercial doctrine, `CQ-` commercial interview; `K-` corporate doctrine, `KQ-` corporate interview; `E-` employment doctrine, `EQ-` employment interview; `P-` paths.

Every jurisdiction-specific equivalent named in a Rationale cell (Saudi, UK, French, Swiss article numbers and agency names) was written from model knowledge during the audit and is `[model knowledge — verify]` until Phase 2 confirms it against the primary text.

The three rows that matter most for the first jurisdiction, because they are silent wrong answers rather than caveats:

- **C-57** `renewal-tracker` hard-codes a Saturday/Sunday weekend and US federal holidays, so cancel-by dates are silently wrong for a Friday/Saturday weekend with lunar-calendar holidays.
- **C-69** `vendor-agreement-review` provisional mode defaults to "US jurisdiction" when the profile is absent.
- **E-87** `log-leave` hard-codes FMLA deadlines (5 business days, 15 days, 75% exhaustion) that the leave-tracker agent itself forbids.

Two gaps where nothing exists to swap and a rule must be authored per jurisdiction: e-signature validity (C-46) and penalty-clause enforceability (C-39, C-70).
