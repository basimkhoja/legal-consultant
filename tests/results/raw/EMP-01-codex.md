**Ground and procedure**

The proposed ordinary route is Article **74(3)**, read with **75**, for employer termination of an indefinite contract. Ahmed’s nationality is consistent with that contract type; the non-national fixed-term rule in Article **37** does not apply. Article **55** supplies the relevant Saudi conversion framework if the original contract history needs reconciliation.  
`labor-law.md`, Arts. 37, 55, 74–75. `[settled — last confirmed 2026-09-04]`

Article 80 is a separate, narrowly conditioned route. Of its nine grounds, only **ground (2)** is a plausible candidate on the stated facts. There is no evidence of assault, dishonesty, deliberate loss, recruitment forgery, abuse of position or disclosure of secrets; probation is expressly excluded. Ground (7) requires qualifying **absence**, not simply a tally of late arrivals. Counsel must assess whether the proven conduct amounts to breach of an essential obligation or disobedience of lawful orders. `[review]`  
[BOE Labor Law, Article 80](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/BoeLaws/Laws/LawDetails/08381293-6388-48e2-8ad2-a9a700f2aa94/1). `[BOE — Arabic]`

Before imposing a dismissal penalty:

- Identify the fresh violation relied on, its discovery date, charge date and investigation completion date. Article 69 imposes separate 30-day clocks.
- Establish the written charge, opportunity to respond and recorded defence under Article 71 and the Article 80 chapeau.
- Check the authorised, proportionate penalty under the certified work regulations.
- Do not impose a second penalty for the same incident. Earlier warnings may support a repetition analysis; they do not replace proof of a later violation.
- Check Article 68’s restriction on aggravating a penalty after 180 days from notification of the earlier penalty.

Sources: `labor-law.md`, Arts. 68–71; `labor-law-implementing-regulations.md`, Annex 1 Arts. 58–70. `[settled — last confirmed 2026-09-04]` Application of the disciplinary clocks to Article 80 dismissal retains the file’s `[model knowledge — verify]` qualification.

**Step 2a — End-of-service award**

**Wage base:** fixed monthly cash housing is included with basic wage. No commission exclusion applies; Article 86 does not permit excluding fixed housing merely by labelling it an allowance.

**Service convention:** calculations count employment through the end of September and use **five completed years plus seven monthly fractions**, assuming uninterrupted Gregorian service. This is an expressly assumed payroll convention, not a claim that Article 84 prescribes that exact day-count method. Counsel/payroll must confirm the contract’s convention before payment. `[review]` `[model knowledge — verify]`

The formula is:

**Award = {0.5 × W × min(S, 5) + W × max(S − 5, 0)} × 1**

The multiplier is **1 for employer termination**. Resignation fractions do not apply. Neither the probation exclusion nor a proven Article 80 exclusion is available on this record.  
`labor-law.md`, Arts. 2, 54, 80, 84–87. `[settled — last confirmed 2026-09-04]`; Article 84 formula also checked against `[BOE — Arabic]`.

**Step 2b — Article 77 compensation**

Article 77 compensation is **conditional exposure**, additional to the award and notice payment where applicable. It becomes relevant if the termination reason is not legitimate or the attempted Article 80 dismissal fails.

For an indefinite contract with no contractual compensation figure:

**Compensation = max(15 × daily actual wage × service years, 2 × monthly actual wage).**

The table shows both completed-year and pro-rata-year results because Article 77 does not expressly resolve fractional years. Use of the last wage in practice and fractional-year treatment retain `[model knowledge — verify]`. Counsel must determine the payable outcome. `[review]`
