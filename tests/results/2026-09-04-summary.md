### Friction noted by the runners (skill text, not failures)

Recorded verbatim at the end of each per-plugin file. The recurring ones:

1. The "no silent supplement" stop after a failed portal fetch says to stop but not what the stopped run must still emit; the runners emitted the reviewer note and options, which is the intended behaviour and should be written into Step 0 item 5.
2. Hijri dates: the vendor review asks for a Hijri date beside Gregorian when the manifest calendar is Hijri; there is no conversion tool in the repo, so the runners tagged the Hijri date `[model knowledge — verify]`. Either drop the instruction or add a converter.
3. `written-consent` cites Companies Law Art. 71 (JSC board conflicts) for LLC conflicts too; the LLC rule should point at the LLC manager rows.
4. `wage-hour-qa` Step 2b says "refuse to compute if an input is missing" while the scenario expects a computation on the stated basic wage; the runner computed on the basic wage and flagged the actual-wage component `[review]`. The step should say which inputs are mandatory and which are flagged.
5. EMP-01's forbidden word "WARN" collides with "warning", a term the Labor Law itself uses; the scenario should say "WARN Act".
6. The `gbr` fixture initially carried `ksa` calendar values in its `## Jurisdiction` section (fixed during the run; the stop rule fired regardless).

None of these changed a verdict. They are queued as Phase 5 fixes to the skill text. Applied on 2026-09-05 (T1), together with the one-line items in the per-plugin observation lists; the one point needing a doctrine answer (LLC partner-manager voting on his own appointment) went to `docs/open-questions-ksa-2026-09-04.md`.
