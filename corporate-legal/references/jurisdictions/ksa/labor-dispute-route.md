## Scope

This file describes the procedural route for an individual labor claim in the Kingdom: the internal grievance for disciplinary penalties, the mandatory friendly-settlement stage administered by HRSD, the labor courts of the Ministry of Justice as the forum of first instance and appeal, the twelve-month limitation and its two statutory exceptions, the prohibition on changing employment terms while a case is pending, the remedies for wage deductions and late wages, and the enforcement of settlement records, judgments and the wage clause of a documented contract. It is written for termination-review, wage-hour-qa and internal-investigation, which need to know what a claimant can do, by when, and what evidence the forum will ask for.

It does not cover: the Law of Procedure before the Sharia Courts and the labor-court circuit structure (not provided), collective disputes beyond the settlement-stage representation rule in Rules Art. 17, the domestic-worker settlement rules (a separate HRSD instrument listed on its page), criminal complaints (harassment, human trafficking), or the Board of Grievances route for HRSD administrative penalties beyond what Reg. Art. 38 says. The friendly-settlement rules were read on 2026-09-05 from an OCR of the scanned HRSD PDF; the rows cite them as "Rules Art. n" and quote the OCR text, with numerals checked against the page image. The Ministry of Justice filing procedures the rules refer to were not read.

## Open questions for a local practitioner

- The friendly-settlement rules (Decision 91285 of 20/6/1445H) were read on 2026-09-05; what remains open: the meaning of "working day" (يوم عمل) in Rules Arts. 12–13 and whether Friday and Saturday are excluded; the Official Gazette issue in which the decision was published (its commencement date under item (fourth)); the Ministry of Justice filing procedures the rules refer to (Arts. 11, 13, 22), in particular whether the court requires the Art. 22 failure record; whether the settlement filing interrupts the Art. 234 twelve months (Art. 4 only says the lapse does not stop the settlement stage); and the mapping of Rules Art. 26's reference to Art. 9(3) of the 1433H Enforcement Law onto the 1447H Law once in force.
- Labor-court structure and appeal: the instruments establishing the labor courts and labor appeal circuits, the appeal window, and whether Supreme Court review is available on labor matters (nothing in the sources read).
- Employment arbitration after the repeal of old Art. 224: whether an arbitration clause in the additional terms of a Qiwa contract is enforceable or void under Annex 5 cl. 14.5 and Art. 8. Rules Art. 4 (read 2026-09-05) says only that such a clause does not stop the settlement stage; the court's treatment remains open.
- Direct enforcement of the wage clause: the enabling instrument (a Council of Ministers decision under Enforcement Law item (h), a ministerial framework, or the contract clause alone), the phasing by contract type, the reported 6 August 2026 extension to open-ended contracts, and the employer's procedural defences.
- The 1433H Enforcement Law (still in force until about 28 October 2026): confirm its executive-instruments list matches the summary above and whether an HRSD settlement record is enforced directly or needs court endorsement.
- Whether Art. 235's freeze on employment terms begins at the friendly-settlement filing or only at the court filing.
- Whether the Art. 72 30-day objection window is treated by the labor courts as a bar to a later Art. 234 claim challenging a dismissal.
- Any limitation on HRSD's power to record and fine violations after the repeal of old Art. 222(3).
