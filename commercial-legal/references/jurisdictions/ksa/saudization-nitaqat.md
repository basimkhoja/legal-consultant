## Scope

Nitaqat is the mechanism by which HRSD gives effect to the Labor Law's Saudization duty: it classifies every private-sector "entity" (all branches of one firm in one economic activity) into a colour range by comparing its Saudization rate with activity- and size-specific thresholds, and switches ministry services on or off by range (new visas, work-permit issuance and renewal, occupation change, transfer-in of expatriate workers). It applies to every establishment with at least one worker and every economic activity in Annex 1 of the guide. The 75% statutory floor in Labor Law Art. 26(2) is not the operative number: the Minister's reduction power is exercised through the Nitaqat thresholds, which range from under 5% to over 90% depending on activity. Profession-specific Saudization decisions (accounting, engineering, pharmacy, dentistry, sales and customer-service roles, and others) sit alongside Nitaqat and are not in the source set. Domestic workers, government employees, and GCC nationals (who are not "expatriate employees" for the guide's formula) are outside the count.

## Open questions for a local practitioner

- **Current Nitaqat coefficients.** The only primary text available is the 2021 guide with 2023-2024 values. Obtain HRSD's 2026-2028 phase decision and Annex (Arabic) and replace the Annex 1 row; confirm whether the y = m·ln(x) + c mechanism was retained.
- **The "Yellow band removed" claim.** The 2021 guide already lists five ranges without Yellow. Establish what the April 2026 announcement actually changed.
- **Qiwa-documentation counting rule (15 April 2026).** Confirm the instrument, the effective date, whether it applies to contracts signed before that date, and whether GOSI-registered Saudis without a Qiwa-documented contract are removed from the numerator only or from both numerator and denominator.
- **Counting rules and multipliers** (SAR 4,000 / 3,000 wage thresholds, disability multiplier, students, part-time one-half, remote workers, owners and partners). None was in the source set; the Regulation confirms only the part-time and disability principles.
- **Averaging window** (26 weeks in practice) and what "immediate counting" for Green-and-above entities means operationally.
- **Profession-specific localisation decisions in force in 2026** (accounting, engineering, pharmacy, dentistry, technical roles and others): decision numbers, phases, thresholds and how Qiwa enforces them at permit renewal.
- **Revised penalties schedule (25 February 2026)** and how fake-Saudization penalties interact with Social Insurance Law Art. 59(2).
- **Treatment of GCC nationals, owners/partners, and seconded (Ajeer) workers** in numerator and denominator.
- **Entity grouping for corporate groups**: whether HRSD still permits "combined entities" treatment (Annex 1 lists coefficients for it) and how a change of commercial-register ownership affects the entity's Nitaqat history.

- `hiring-review` (employment-legal), at the eligibility-and-authorisation step: cite Labor Law Arts. 32-35 and the Services-by-Range row before advising that an expatriate hire is feasible; ask for the hiring entity's current Nitaqat band from Qiwa (dated) and refuse to infer it from the Annex 1 coefficients. For an entity under the 2026 Qiwa-documentation rule, point to `platform-obligations.md` for the documentation duty.
- `worker-classification` (employment-legal): cite the labour-services row (seconded workers count in the client's ratio unless the ministry sets conditions) when an outsourcing or secondment structure is being assessed; cite Regulation Art. 14 First item 16 (no recruitment or transfer without actual work) via `platform-obligations.md`.
- `entity-compliance` (corporate-legal): list "Nitaqat band per entity (Qiwa export, dated)", "profession-specific localisation compliance", and "Art. 28 disability quota statement" as periodic checks; cite Regulation Art. 8 for the service-withholding consequence.
- `diligence-issue-extraction` and `tabular-review` (corporate-legal): extract, per Saudi entity, the Nitaqat band and rate, the number of Saudis needed for the next band, any recruitment suspension under Regulation Art. 14 First item 12, and the reconciliation between GOSI Saudis, Qiwa-documented Saudi contracts and payroll. A Red or Low Green band in a target dependent on expatriate work permits is a material finding; cite the Services-by-Range row and Labor Law Art. 33.
- `vendor-agreement-review` (commercial-legal) for manpower and outsourcing contracts: cite the labour-services row and ask for the Nitaqat treatment of supplied workers.
