## Scope

The Law applies to every establishment (natural or legal person carrying on economic activity) inside the Kingdom and to conduct outside the Kingdom with an anti-competitive effect inside it (Art. 3). It prohibits anti-competitive agreements (Art. 5) and abuse of dominance (Art. 6), and imposes mandatory, suspensory pre-completion notification of economic concentrations above a turnover threshold set by the Regulations (Arts. 7, 9–11). State-owned entities are excluded only where solely authorised by the government to supply a product or service (Art. 3(2)). This file is written for the merger-control diligence checklist; Arts. 5, 6 and the exemption mechanism are covered as notes only. Sector rules (CMA for listed-company mergers, SAMA, CST) run alongside and are not covered. The Regulations were not read; where the Guidelines quote them the quotation is used.

## Open questions for a local practitioner

- **Current text of the Implementing Regulations and the threshold decision.** The Regulations (Board Res. 337 of 25/1/1441H) were not read. Confirm whether Reg. Art. 12 now contains the three cumulative criteria in its own text or whether they rest on a Board decision under Reg. Art. 12(3), the decision number, its publication and its effective date, and whether the SAR 200m / 40m / 40m figures have been revised since Guidelines v5 (April 2025).
- **Fee basis.** Confirm that the 0.02% / SAR 250,000 fee is in the current Regulations and whether it is calculated on worldwide or Saudi sales of the participating undertakings (the Guidelines say the same basis as the threshold, i.e. worldwide).
- **Fine base for gun-jumping.** Art. 19(1) fixes the fine at up to 10% of "the annual sales value that is the subject of the violation" (محل المخالفة). Ask how GAC and the Committee have computed this in published gun-jumping decisions — target sales, Saudi sales or combined group sales.
- **Deemed approval in practice.** Art. 11(2) allows completion after 90 days of silence; the Guidelines start the clock from the completeness notice and stop it for information requests. Confirm whether GAC issues formal completeness notices and whether any party has closed on deemed approval.
- **Simplified or short-form procedure.** The Guidelines read today contain no short-form filing for no-overlap or foreign-to-foreign transactions; confirm whether a simplified procedure or a fee reduction exists in the Regulations or GAC practice.
- **Appeal from a refusal or conditional approval.** The Law provides a 30-day appeal from Committee penalty decisions (Art. 18(3)) but no specific route for Board decisions under Art. 10; confirm the forum and time limit.
- **Interaction with CMA and sector regulators.** For listed targets, the CMA's Merger and Acquisition Regulations run in parallel (Companies Law Art. 274 gives the CMA the merger role for listed companies); confirm sequencing and whether GAC clearance is required before the CMA offer document.
- **Arabic Guidelines.** The Guidelines state that only the Arabic version is authentic; the Arabic v5 was not read. Any divergence between the English summary rows above and the Arabic must be resolved in favour of the Arabic.
