> **Which law applies (matter-date rule).** Two sets of rows follow. For a tender, award, contract, variation, penalty, termination or grievance whose operative date is **on or after the 1448H in-force date** (2027-01-02, `[review]` on that day; certain from 2027-01-03), cite the **GTPL 1448H** rows. For an operative date **up to 2027-01-01**, cite the **Repealed law (M/128 of 1440H)** rows. A contract signed under the 2019 law and still running after the in-force date: the 1448H law contains no transitional article on running contracts (Arts. 98–101 cover only the regulations, replacement and entry into force), so cite the 2019 rows for the contract's terms, flag `[review — transition to the 1448H law not settled in the text; check the Implementing Regulations when issued]`, and do not supplement. The two laws renumber almost every article; never cite an article number without saying which law it belongs to. Nothing in either set is tagged `[BOE — Arabic]` for the 1448H law until the portal lists it.

## Scope

The 1448H Law binds "government entities": ministries, government agencies, authorities, public departments and institutions, and bodies with independent public legal personality (Art. 1), for all their works and procurement (Art. 10), with the Regulations to set special rules for works and procurement executed outside the Kingdom (Art. 11) and for dealings with persons licensed outside the Kingdom (Art. 3(3)). Companies that execute works and procurement *on behalf of* government entities must apply the Law and the Regulations, but the Regulations may exempt them from some provisions (Art. 95, new). Government entities may act for one another in contracting and contract administration (Art. 89(2)). The Law does not, on its own terms, bind state-owned or PIF-owned commercial companies contracting for their own account (stc, Saudi Aramco, SABIC, Ma'aden, SEC and similar are not "government entities" under Art. 1); the 2019 text's express exemption of companies at least 51% state-owned from the performance bond, which was the clearest textual sign that such companies are contractors rather than procuring entities, is **not** carried into Art. 59(2) of the 1448H Law, so that inference is now weaker and the position rests on Art. 1 alone `[model knowledge — verify]`; confirm per counterparty whether it acts on behalf of a government entity (Art. 95). The Law governs the government side of the relationship: award method, guarantees, price and payment, variations, penalties, withdrawal and termination, and the complaint route. A subcontractor is reached only through the government's consent to the subcontract and the prime's joint liability (Art. 69); the prime's own obligations bind it through the mandatory model contracts (Art. 93). Sale of government movables (Arts. 78–83) is inside the Law; leasing and investment of government real estate are not addressed on the gazette page.
