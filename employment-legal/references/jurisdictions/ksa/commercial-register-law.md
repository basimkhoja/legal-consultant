## Scope

The Law governs the single national commercial register (السجل التجاري), defined as a central electronic database of merchants' data (Art. 1), and applies to every "merchant" (تاجر), natural or juristic, who meets that description under the Commercial Law (النظام التجاري) (Art. 1, 5). It regulates registration, the data recorded, updating, annual confirmation, suspension and deletion, public access, the evidentiary effect of registered data, and penalties. It does not itself regulate trade names (see `trade-names-law.md`), company formation or governance (Companies Law), or foreign-investment licensing (Investment Law); those instruments are expressly preserved (Art. 6, 9, 13). The Law sets no expiry or renewal cycle for a registration: the operative concepts are a one-off registration (Art. 5), a 15-day duty to update (Art. 10), and an annual confirmation of data (Art. 11), backed by suspension (Art. 15). The five-year transition for legacy branch ("sub") registers is in the Royal Decree itself, not in the Law's articles.

## Open questions for a local practitioner

- The Implementing Regulation (issued by the Minister of Commerce under Art. 28) was not read. It governs: the exempt categories of merchant (Art. 5(2)), the post-registration bank-account data (Art. 6(2)), the due date and form of the annual confirmation (Art. 11), the "basic" versus "detailed" data split for public access (Art. 18), the offence classification table (Art. 21(3)), and fees (Art. 27). Every row above that says "as set by the Regulation" is incomplete without it.
- Effective date: Art. 29 says "after 180 days" from publication on 4 October 2024. Day 180 is 2 April 2025; the Ministry's announced go-live of 3 April 2025 is model knowledge. Confirm which date the Ministry and courts treat as the first day in force, because the five-year branch-CR deadline (Royal Decree item Third) runs from it.
- Branch registrations: the Law's articles do not use the term "sub-register" or state a "one CR per merchant" rule in terms; the single-register model is carried by the Art. 1 definition (one central database), Art. 9 (multiple activities on one registration) and the Royal Decree's five-year strike-off of all sub-registers. Confirm with counsel how the Ministry treats a company with branches in several cities after the transition (branch addresses recorded on the single CR, or something else) and what the "mechanism set by the Ministry" requires.
- The Law says nothing about a registration "expiry". Confirm that the Ministry's platform has stopped printing expiry dates on extracts and that a bank or government counterparty will accept an extract without one.
- Art. 20's list of court notifications does not include enforcement (Enforcement Law) measures against the merchant; confirm whether enforcement-court restrictions appear on the CR or only on the enforcement portal.
- Art. 15(6): the Ministry "may" seek dissolution of a company whose suspension is not cured within a year; practice on whether and when it does so is unknown.

- `escalation-flagger`, `stakeholder-summary`: cite Art. 21 for the maximum fine when quantifying a registration-compliance finding.
