## Scope

The Law governs all investment (local and foreign) in the Kingdom: it defines investment and capital, grants investor rights (equal treatment, fair and equitable treatment, protection against confiscation and expropriation, free transfer of funds), creates a National Register of Investors kept by MISA, and replaces the foreign-investment *licence* of the 2000 Foreign Investment Law with *registration* of the foreign investor before investing (Art. 7). It does not itself list the activities closed or restricted to foreign investors — that list is issued by the Examination Committee and published in the Investor Guide (Art. 8; Reg. Art. 15), and neither was among the sources read. It does not apply to investment in securities governed by the Capital Market Law (Art. 7(2)), and it leaves sector-specific regimes and special economic zones intact subject to a floor of the rights in the Law (Art. 14). Tax, zakat, Saudization and company-law obligations are outside it (Art. 5 simply requires compliance with all laws). Penalties are limited to breaches of the registration and excluded-activities articles (Art. 11).

## Open questions for a local practitioner

- **Regulations issuing instrument.** The MISA English text of the Regulations shows no decision number or date. Confirm the Ministerial Decision number and date (understood to be No. 1086 of 7 February 2025), confirm the Official Gazette publication, and obtain the Arabic text — the English rendering is MISA's, and the Bureau of Experts has not published an official translation of the Regulations.
- **Who is a "foreign investor" when the investor is a Saudi company with foreign shareholders?** Art. 1 defines the local investor by Saudi nationality of the person investing. Whether a Saudi-incorporated LLC with a 10% non-GCC shareholder must itself register, and at what ownership level registration is triggered, is a MISA practice rule in the Investor Guide, not in the Law.
- **The excluded-activities list.** The list and its current version were not read. Confirm the current list, its issue date, which activities are "prohibited" versus "restricted", and the conditions attached to restricted activities (Saudi participation, capital, experience), since Reg. Art. 30(b) makes breach of those conditions a serious violation.
- **Minimum capital.** Neither the Law nor the Regulations set minimum capital. Confirm whether MISA still applies capital thresholds to particular restricted activities (e.g. trading, real estate, engineering consultancy) through the Investor Guide, and whether these have been withdrawn.
- **Migration of pre-2025 licence-holders.** Decree para. 2 preserves acquired rights until their terms expire. Confirm how MISA converted existing licences into registrations, whether the annual-update deadline for converted investors runs from the old licence anniversary, and whether the old annual licence fee survives in any form.
- **Timing of Examination Committee approvals.** Reg. Arts. 16–18 set deadlines for MISA's notifications but none for the Committee's decision on an excluded-activity application or an Art. 8(3) ownership change. Ask for typical turnaround so the closing-checklist critical path can be estimated.
- **Appeal forum for national-security suspensions.** Reg. Art. 23 says the investor may appeal "in accordance with the laws in force" without naming the forum or the period; Art. 12's 30-day appeal applies in terms only to Ministry *penalty* decisions.
- **Interaction with the Anti-Concealment Law and the Companies Law branch rules.** Operating without registration is a serious violation here; confirm how MISA and the Ministry of Commerce coordinate on nominee-shareholder cases and whether a branch of a foreign company (Companies Law Arts. 235–239) registers as an investor in the same way as a subsidiary.
