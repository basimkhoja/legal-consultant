## Scope

The law establishes three social-insurance branches administered by GOSI: the annuities (pensions) branch (فرع المعاشات), the occupational hazards and additional compensations branch (فرع الأخطار المهنية والتعويضات الإضافية), and the unemployment insurance branch (فرع التأمين ضد التعطل عن العمل, marketed as SANED). Every natural or legal person who employs one or more workers or employees for a wage is an "employer" (Art. 1). The annuities and unemployment branches are compulsory for Saudi workers and, for the first time, Saudi public-sector employees (the new law merges the civil retirement and social insurance schemes for new entrants); the occupational hazards branch is compulsory for all workers and employees in the Kingdom regardless of nationality (Art. 28). The law does not apply to anyone who had contribution periods under the 1421H law or the Civil Retirement Law before 3 July 2024, or to existing pensioners: those people remain under the 1421H law (contribution 9% + 9%, retirement at 60) subject to the phased retirement-age and qualifying-period tables in Decree clause Fifth. Practically, every private employer runs two populations side by side: pre-July 2024 hires on the 1421H rates, and later hires on the new law's rising rates. Not covered here: the Implementing Regulation (registration deadlines, wage elements, exemptions from fines), the Civil Retirement Law, and GOSI's optional savings programs (Art. 4).

## Open questions for a local practitioner

- **Exact step dates for the annuities phase-in.** Decree clause Third(1) fixes 18% "during the twelve months from the effective date" and 19% "from the month following the lapse of twelve months". Counting from 3 July 2024, the twelve months lapse on 2 July 2025 and the "following month" is August 2025; GOSI's public communications applied the 9.5% + 9.5% step from July 2025. Confirm which month GOSI billed and whether the 2026 step (10% + 10%) was applied from July or August 2026.
- **Which penalty regime applies to legacy-contributor violations after 3 July 2024** (new Art. 59, SAR 50,000 per violation with per-contributor multiplier, or 1421H Art. 62 as amended by M/55 of 1438H, SAR 10,000 per violation with per-contributor and per-data-item multipliers). Decree clause Fourth continues the 1421H "provisions" for legacy contributors without carving out enforcement.
- **Which paragraph of 1421H Art. 38 was deleted by M/134 of 1440H.** The portal note records the deletion without naming the paragraph; the row above reads it as para 1(c) (women's pension at 55), consistent with M/134's amendment of Labor Law Art. 74(4). Confirm.
- **Interaction of GOSI maternity compensation (Arts. 41-42) with the employer's paid maternity leave under Labor Law Art. 151 as amended by M/44 of 1446H.** The law bars combining compensation with wages; whether the employer's wage obligation is displaced, topped up, or reimbursed is a Regulation and GOSI-practice question. Council of Ministers Decision 1022 clause Eleventh directed an HRSD-led committee to review, within 90 days, the maternity and childbirth leave provisions of the Labor Law and other laws' retirement-age provisions for alignment with the new law; whether that review produced an instrument was not verifiable from the sources.
- **Contributory wage elements under the new Implementing Regulation.** Art. 8 fixes only "basic wage" and delegates the rest; confirm whether housing allowance (and the two-months-per-year valuation of in-kind housing) and commissions carry over from the 1421H Regulation.
- **Registration and payment deadlines** (Art. 7, Art. 9): the 15-day figures used in practice come from the 1421H law and GOSI practice; confirm the new Regulation's periods and the Board's threshold for suspending period-counting (not less than six months, Art. 9(4)).
- **Board decisions under Art. 6** switching coverage on for domestic workers, sub-60-day visa holders, and sports players and coaches; and any Council decision under Art. 5 creating compulsory products for non-Saudi workers.
- **Whether GOSI's per-contributor "ghost registration" fine (Art. 59(2)) is being applied cumulatively with HRSD's fake-Saudization penalties** for the same facts, and GOSI's settlement practice under Art. 59(6).
- **SANED rate for legacy contributors.** The 0.75% + 0.75% used above for the legacy group rests on the Unemployment Insurance Law of 1435H as amended, which was not in the source set; Decision 1022 fixes 1.5% only for new-law contributors.
