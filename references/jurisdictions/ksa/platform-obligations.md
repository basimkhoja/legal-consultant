## Scope

This file identifies the points at which the Saudi labour-administration platforms are the legally required channel for an employer's obligation, so that a reviewer can distinguish "the contract is valid but undocumented" from "the employer is in breach". It covers corporate (private-sector) employers under the Labor Law: establishment registration, employment-contract documentation on Qiwa, work-permit issuance and renewal, occupation change, transfer of services (نقل خدمات) including the cases where a non-Saudi worker may move without the employer's consent, wage payment through the Wage Protection System via Mudad, GOSI registration and wage declarations, and exit/re-entry and final-exit visas. Musaned (domestic workers) is noted for completeness only; domestic workers are outside the Labor Law (Art. 7) and outside the scope of the consuming skills. The file does not cover platform fees, Qiwa subscription terms, or the Ministry of Interior's residency rules beyond what the Labor Law says.

## Open questions for a local practitioner

- **The HRSD schedule of violations and penalties under Art. 230(2)-(3)** in force in 2026 (MD 178743 of 1440H as revised, reportedly from 25 February 2026): the graduated amounts for undocumented contracts, expired permits, occupation mismatch, cash wages and WPS non-compliance, and which violations sit in the court-only schedule under Art. 230(3).
- **Art. 35 as amended: the Regulation procedures "ensuring the worker is not harmed"** by a refusal to renew, and how Qiwa operationalises the no-consent transfer for workers of a non-compliant employer.
- **Art. 30 as amended: the scope of "outsourcing activity" (نشاط الإسناد)** requiring an HRSD licence, and whether intra-group secondments or client-site placements by ordinary companies fall within it (Art. 229 bis exposure of SAR 200,000-500,000).
- **The instrument that makes Qiwa the "approved platform"** for contract documentation, transfers, occupation changes and recruitment: the Regulation says "المنصة المعتمدة لدى الوزارة" without naming Qiwa; confirm the HRSD decision that designates it and the deadline for documenting contracts of employees hired before the Regulation.
- **WPS decisions**: the current HRSD decision(s) establishing the Wage Protection Program, the compliance-percentage thresholds, the grace periods for non-upload, the current fine amounts, and the treatment of employees on unpaid leave, secondment or notice.
- **Penalties schedule**: the ministerial decision in force (MD 178743 of 1440H as revised, reportedly from 25 February 2026) and its size-graduated amounts for undocumented contracts, expired permits, occupation mismatch and WPS non-compliance.
- **Wage-clause direct enforcement** (phase 3 from 6 August 2026, as claimed): confirm the joint HRSD-MoJ framework, which contract types are covered, and whether the employer can use it against the worker.
- **Transfer-without-consent practice**: whether Qiwa applies the twelve-month / ninety-day route (Regulation Art. 14 Second item 21) automatically or requires a ministry decision; whether Art. 77 compensation is enforced against a transferring worker in practice; whether item 15's "may not be conditioned" defeats settlement agreements that trade the release for a waiver.
- **Absher/Muqeem rules** on worker-initiated exit/re-entry and final exit, and the employer's notice rights.
- **Integration effects**: what a Qiwa-GOSI wage mismatch triggers, and whether the Ministry of Commerce blocks commercial-register renewal for GOSI or WPS non-compliance automatically.
- **Musaned/Domestic Workers Regulation**: confirmed out of scope; no question unless an executive package includes household staff.
